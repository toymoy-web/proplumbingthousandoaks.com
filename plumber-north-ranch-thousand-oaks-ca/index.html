Plumbing Help In North Ranch

North Ranch sits in the 91362 ZIP code near Kanan Road and Upper Ranch Road. The area was unincorporated until 1973, when Thousand Oaks approved annexing it, and it borders Oak Park. We take plumbing calls here for leaks, water heaters, drains and fixture repair.

The mailing address question is real. Listings for North Ranch often read Westlake Village, and ZIP 91362 is shared between Thousand Oaks and Westlake Village. A shared ZIP code does not tell you which city a property is in, so confirm your city with your property record if it matters for permits or services.

What We Know About The Area

The Conejo Recreation and Park District runs North Ranch Neighborhood Park at 1901 Upper Ranch Road, at the southwest corner of Kanan Road and Upper Ranch Road, and North Ranch Playfield at 952 Rockfield Street.

Local real estate guides describe more than 300 custom homes built from the late 1970s into the 2000s, along with gated enclaves such as North Ranch Country Club Estates and the Braemar townhome community. Those are brokerage descriptions, so verify details for your own address.

Hillside custom homes are often larger than tract homes, and larger homes tend to have longer pipe runs and more fixtures. That is general plumbing information and not a statement about any specific house.
